# ATHENA OS Feature Map

## Athlete Command Center
- Local athlete profile and Event Genome
- Event-specific readiness weights
- Explainable score factors
- Health-flag safety caps
- Personalized best-fit session
- Voice summary
- Readiness history chart
- Session Compatibility pressure test

## Universal Event Lab
- Sprint race blueprint: 60m, 100m, 200m, 300m, 400m
- Hurdle rhythm engine: 100H, 110H, 300H, 400H
- Distance pace matrix: 800m, 1600m, 3200m, 5K
- Jump series analytics: LJ, TJ, HJ, PV
- Throw series analytics: shot put, discus, javelin, hammer
- Relay optimizer: 4×100, 4×200, 4×400, 4×800
- All 24 four-athlete orders evaluated

## Athlete Health Hub
- Heat awareness planner
- Heart-rate zone education
- Seven-night sleep bank
- Heat, concussion, breathing, cardiac, and serious-injury warning education

## Nursing Communication
- SBAR-style athlete handoff
- Symptom checklist
- Emergency escalation banner
- Copy and printable output

## Athlete Research Studio
- Research question and protocol builder
- Safety boundary
- Observation log
- Trend graph
- CSV export

## Product Design
- Responsive desktop and mobile UI
- Light and dark themes
- Offline-capable PWA
- Browser-local data storage
- Accessibility support
- Reduced-motion support
- No external libraries required
