# ATHENA OS — Co-Founder Project Pitch

## One-sentence pitch

ATHENA OS is a privacy-first athlete intelligence platform that adapts to every track and field discipline while connecting performance planning, recovery awareness, safety education, nursing-style communication, and athlete-led research.

## The problem

Track and field is treated like one sport, but a 100m sprinter, 3200m runner, pole vaulter, shot putter, and relay anchor have completely different demands. Most student tools either track generic workouts or focus on one event. Health concerns are also often described vaguely, making it harder for athletes to communicate clearly with a coach, parent, school nurse, or athletic trainer.

## The solution

ATHENA creates a local Event Genome for each athlete and changes its tools based on the athlete’s discipline. It combines:

- Event-specific readiness weighting
- Session compatibility analysis
- Sprint, hurdle, distance, jump, throw, and relay tools
- A 24-order relay optimizer
- Heat, sleep, and heart-rate education
- Emergency warning education
- An SBAR-style athlete-to-care handoff
- A research studio with CSV export

## Why it is original

ATHENA is not another generic fitness tracker and does not copy AAINA’s camera-based dance mirror. Its defining idea is the bridge between **every track and field event**, **athlete health awareness**, and **clear nursing-inspired communication**.

## Responsible design

- All athlete information remains in the browser.
- The app works offline after first load.
- The readiness model explains its factors.
- Health flags override performance recommendations.
- The app does not diagnose, predict injuries, or clear participation.
- Emergency warnings tell athletes to stop and seek adult or emergency help.

## 60-second demo

1. Choose an event such as 400m, long jump, or discus.
2. Change sleep, soreness, hydration, and health flags to show the event-specific readiness response.
3. Pressure-test a planned practice with the Session Compatibility tool.
4. Open Event Lab and show that each discipline has a different interface.
5. In Relay Intelligence, evaluate all 24 possible team orders.
6. Create an urgent SBAR handoff and show the emergency escalation banner.
7. End with the Research Studio and equal About the Founders section.

## Future validation roadmap

The next step is not to make stronger medical claims. It is to work with qualified school nurses, athletic trainers, coaches, and student athletes to test usability, communication quality, accessibility, and safety.
